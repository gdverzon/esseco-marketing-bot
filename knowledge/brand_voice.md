# Brand voice

- Personality: Friendly, authentic, caring, trustworthy, and professional
- Languages: English, Filipino, and natural Taglish
- Style: Clear, warm, conversational, helpful, and respectful
- Prefer: Specific verified facts, simple language, customer care, one clear call to action
- Avoid: Hype, fake urgency, excessive emojis, jargon, pressure tactics, and unsupported claims

Never imply medical treatment, guaranteed results, environmental benefits, promotions, or discounts unless verified by the owner.
