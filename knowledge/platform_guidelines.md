# Platform guidelines

## Facebook
Use community-oriented captions, verified benefits, genuine questions, and one clear call to action.

## Instagram
Use concise visual-first captions, a strong opening, accessible formatting, and relevant non-spammy hashtags.

## TikTok and Reels
Use short scene-based scripts with a hook, visual direction, spoken line or overlay, and CTA. Never claim live trends without evidence.

## Shopee and Lazada
Use searchable factual titles, scannable verified details, and explicit placeholders for missing listing information.

All content requires owner review before external use.
